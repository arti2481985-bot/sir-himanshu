Meet Sir Himanshu

Sir Himanshu is the person behind the vision of this Tabela. His approach is built around care, responsibility and respect for animals.

For him, a Tabela is more than simply a place where cattle are kept. It is a place where cleanliness, quality and dedication come together.

His vision is to create a responsible environment where traditional values and modern practices work together for better animal care.
