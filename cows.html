Gir

The Gir is a famous Indian cattle breed known for its distinctive long ears, curved horns, adaptability and good milk-producing ability.

Approx. Market Price

Sahiwal

Sahiwal cattle are valued as an Indian dairy breed. They are generally reddish in colour and are well adapted to hot climates.

Approx. Market Price
₹40,000 – ₹1,20,000
03

Red Sindhi

Red Sindhi cattle are recognised by their reddish colour and compact body. The breed is widely known for its adaptability and dairy characteristics.

Approx. Market Price
₹38,000 – ₹70,000
04

Tharparkar

Tharparkar cattle generally have a white or light-grey appearance and are known for their ability to perform well in dry and hot regions.

Approx. Market Price
₹35,000 – ₹80,000
05

Rathi

Rathi cattle are an important breed from Rajasthan. They are valued for their milk production and their ability to tolerate demanding climatic conditions.

Approx. Market Price
₹45,000 – ₹1,20,000
06

Kankrej

Kankrej cattle are a strong Indian breed known for their characteristic lyre-shaped horns, hardiness and usefulness for both milk and agricultural work.

Approx. Market Price
₹50,000 – ₹1,50,000
07

Ongole

Ongole cattle are a large Indian breed from Andhra Pradesh. They are recognised for their strength, size and adaptability to hot conditions.

Approx. Market Price
₹60,000 – ₹1,50,000
08

Hariana

Hariana cattle are a well-known northern Indian breed. They are generally white or light grey and are valued for their strength and utility.

Approx. Market Price
₹35,000 – ₹80,000
09

Deoni

Deoni cattle are an Indian dual-purpose breed known for their balanced utility, adaptability and suitability for both milk production and farm work.

Approx. Market Price
₹40,000 – ₹1,00,000

Hallikar

Hallikar is a traditional Indian cattle breed recognised especially for its strength, endurance and suitability for agricultural work.

Approx. Market Price
₹40,000 – ₹1,00,000
